Pack Smart

You have a bag with a weight limit and a pile of items. Which ones should go in? Enter your own items and compare five ways to decide, then see the pickup trip for the packed items on a map.

The problemThe 0/1 Knapsack Problem: each item has a weight and a value. Pick a set whose total weight fits the limit and whose total value is as high as possible. Each item is taken whole or not at all.
Why it mattersLoading delivery vans, choosing ads for a budget, picking projects for limited hours, cutting cargo for a flight: any “best use of limited space” is a knapsack.
What the program computesFor your items and limit: the chosen items, total weight, total value, and how much work each algorithm did, checked against the best value found.

Your items

Weights and values are whole numbers from 1 to 999. Capacity is a whole number from 1 to 1000. Up to 15 items.

Pickup map

Each item sits somewhere on a 10 km × 7 km map. The depot (D) must drive to every packed item and back. Orange pins are in the bag, grey pins are left behind. Drag any pin to move it and watch the distance change.
