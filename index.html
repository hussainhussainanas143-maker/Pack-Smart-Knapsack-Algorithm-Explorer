Why it matters in real life

Every time something valuable has to fit into limited space, weight or budget, someone is solving a knapsack problem. Better answers mean less waste, lower cost and more value from the same resources.

  • Delivery vans and cargoChoose which parcels to load so the van carries the most value without going over its weight limit.
  • Budgets and adsPick the campaigns or purchases that give the biggest return for a fixed budget.
  • Projects and timeDecide which tasks fit into a limited number of working hours with the highest payoff.
  • Travel and backpackingPack the most useful gear under an airline or hiking weight limit.
